      Memories

      Eight places to keep the knobs as they are now. Fantasy and the Reality Synth keep their own.

        The Reality Synth

        Press the glowing red display on the right panel (or Reality synth in the bar). The ambience, his voice and every secret go quiet and the console becomes a real synthesizer. Coloured keys A to P are sixteen patches, Q to W set the key for the chord pads. Knobs: cutoff, resonance, reverb (cream row); attack (red), release (green); octave (blue); tempo (lower cream); detune (bottom left); chord (bottom gold); delay (bottom right); sweep and vibrato (the knobs beside him); shimmer (the small black knob). Red switches: mono glide, sustain. Blue: delay, reverb, arpeggio; yellow: arp pattern. The three switches by the record button: sub, chorus, octave layer. The sun dial: pump. The twelve pads: chords. The red button top left: tap tempo; the grey one: all notes off. The first jack: MIDI keyboard. Drag the crank for pitch bend. Computer keys: the whole keyboard is a piano. Z to / is the lower octave (black keys on S D, G H J, L ;), Q to ] the upper octave (black keys on 2 3, 5 6 7, 9 0, =). Left and right arrows: octave. Up and down: patches. Space: sustain. Shift + 1 to 9: chords. Press the red display again to go back.

        Keys

        Play the keyboard with your mouse, or type it like a piano. The middle letter row A S D F G H J K L ; ' plays the white keys, and W E, T Y U, O P above it play the black keys. Z and X (or the left and right arrows) move the octave; C and V make you softer or harder. Every letter you type is also spelled on the board.

        Spelling

        The planchette follows your letters. Space ends a word. Some words do things. Find them.

        Knobs

        Drag up or down, or scroll. The cream row: tone, shimmer, space. Red: drone. Green: trance (the beat between your ears). Blue: root (each planet has its own). Lower cream: pulse.

        Buttons

        Red switches: rite and freeze. Blue switches: echo, room, arpeggio. Yellow: dice. Round red: record a loop. The gold sun dial: journey. The pads on the right: his sounds. The eight buttons bottom left: grooves (Drift Phonk, Memphis Phonk, Half-Time Trap, Witch House, Séance Drill, Dust Boom Bap, Ritual Drums, Ambient Pulse): tap one to play it in tempo and key, tap another to switch on the next bar, tap the lit one to stop. Memories (in the top bar): keep the knobs as they are and bring them back. The crystal: strike it.

        The coloured keys

        Each one is a sound for the whole keyboard. Press it to hear it and play it on the keys. Many carry a binaural beat: each ear gets a slightly different pitch and you hear the difference as a slow pulse, the old entrainment. Some retune the keyboard to the “angelic” frequencies (396, 528, 639, 741, 852, 963) or A = 432: a modern tradition, beautiful either way. Headphones, or you won’t hear the beats.

        The Spell Loom

        The seal at the top of the console, or Spells in the bar. Weave a hypnosis spell from an intent, a voice, a depth, a length and a carrier. Cast it, keep it, share its code. Escape breaks a spell.

        Hidden things

        Every control does more than one thing. Tap is the obvious one. Hold does something else. Twice does something else again. Some things only happen in combination. The Discoveries book keeps count.

        A note

        The beats need headphones. This is an instrument, not a medical treatment. Press H to see every control outlined.

        The Parlour

        His console, all of it, playable. Keys, knobs, switches, screens, the board, the eye. Headphones on. Don't be lazy.
