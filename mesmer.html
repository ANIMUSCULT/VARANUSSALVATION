The Trance

Hold the cordspace bar, or press and hold anywhere

The magnetic tub

a trance you run yourself

Up to three. You’ll hear nothing you didn’t pick here.

Have it in your hand when you start. It sits in the tub with you.

Binaural needs headphones. Everything follows the volume up top.

This is a trance. You stay in charge. Wake, or the Escape key, brings you up gently at any second.

No flashing. Every light in here changes slowly. If light patterns have ever made you unwell, skip this one.

Not while driving, and not while doing anything that needs you awake. Sound and light, not medicine.

Sit for a moment.

Drink some water. Don’t text anyone yet.
