    Two trumps

      How pairs read

      Two cards make the smallest sentence

      Readers learn pairs first because every bigger spread is pairs talking to each other. Read the first card as the subject and the second as what happens to it, then swap them and listen to the difference.

      The elements

      Wands are fire, Cups water, Swords air, Pentacles earth, and every trump has an element too. Fire and air feed each other, so do water and earth. Fire and water fight, so do air and earth. The same element twice runs strong; the rest sit side by side.

      Trumps and pips

      Two trumps: forces bigger than the asker. A trump beside a numbered card explains it: the trump is the story, the pip is where it shows up in an ordinary day. When the pip has the trump's own number, it is a small echo of it.

      Suits and numbers

      Two cards of one suit keep the whole matter in that suit's world. One step apart, the story moves on. Two of the same number repeat that number's lesson in two worlds.

      Court cards

      A court card is usually a person, sometimes a way of acting. Beside another card, it is someone with a hand in that matter.

      Reversed

      A reversed card pulls against its partner or arrives late. Two reversed: slow down; nothing is flowing yet.

      The book

      Some pairs have been read so often they carry their own meaning: the Tower with the Star, Death with the Sun. The machine shows those first. Every other pair is read by the rules above.
