Welcome to the library

Hundreds of plants, spices, teas, oils, mushrooms, vitamins and old healing practices from around the world, with what people have done with them, the stories behind them, what studies found, and what to be careful about.

To look something up, type in the box at the top: a plant (ginger), a feeling (can’t sleep), a part of the body (sore feet) or a tradition (Ayurveda).

Or touch the room. The big eye dial is the alphabet. The mirror shows today’s plant. The little copper disc on the mat picks a page for you. The candle opens the poison garden.
